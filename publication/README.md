## Contents and reading order

1. `covariogram-uniqueness-candidate.tex`: the English mathematical paper,
   with its detailed proposed proofs in appendices.
2. `lean-verification-supplement.tex`: Supplement S1, documenting the exact
   coverage and limitations of the partial Lean verification.
3. `computational-diagnostics.tex`: Supplement S2, numerical and rational
   diagnostics with their limitations.
4. `covariogram-uniqueness-candidate-1.0-candidate.zip`: the exact Git
   snapshot for this deposit, including the above files, Lean and Python
   sources, recorded checks, research notes, bibliography, licenses and
   historical timestamp evidence. Downloaded dependencies and build caches
   are excluded.
5. `deposit-manifest.json` and `SHA256SUMS`: the full Git commit/tree IDs
   and cryptographic digests of the deposit's content files.

All three current LaTeX documents are standalone, with references embedded
in their sources. Compile with a conventional LaTeX distribution and run
twice to resolve cross-references. This edition contains no exported PDF.
The root README in the archive explains how to reproduce the Lean checks.

## Mathematical status

This is a candidate argument submitted for independent scrutiny, not a
validated solution. Its two central propositions are not fully formalized.
The Lean materials check 33 explicitly stated supporting and conditional
theorems, not the complete covariogram uniqueness theorem. AI assistance
and the author's nonprofessional research context are disclosed in the paper.
